# A collected study

The portfolio pairs ivory paper, oxblood, forest green, aubergine, ochre and aged brass with self-hosted Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans. Framing, textile stripes, layered paper and editorial typography interpret a cozy, high-fashion maximalist home while keeping the focus on Umar's engineering career.

## Decorative image

Final asset: `public/images/study-editorial.webp` (1200 × 800, WebP).

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded and resized for the website. No CLI fallback was used. The image is decorative and does not depict Umar's actual home or workplace. The original portrait of Umar is retained separately and was not generated or altered.

### Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: atmospheric editorial image for a software engineering portfolio website inspired by a cozy, high-fashion, maximalist home. Create one landscape, refined magazine-quality interior photograph, no text and no people. A beautifully collected study corner with a deep oxblood velvet armchair, ochre and olive striped textile cushion, aubergine glazed sculptural ceramic vase on a dark walnut side table, a brass reading lamp, leafy plant, eclectic framed abstract art and stacked art books. Honey sunlight through a window, ivory plaster walls, softly patterned Persian rug, natural materials and rich tactile textures. Warm, lived-in, intelligent, inviting, tasteful layered maximalism with luxury fashion editorial sensibility. Natural balanced colour combinations: ivory and warm beige, oxblood and terracotta red, forest green, aubergine purple, ochre yellow and aged gold. Composition: horizontal 3:2 crop, coherent photographic scene, artfully arranged rather than cluttered, sophisticated warm shadows, visible fabric and wood grain. Absolutely no letters, typography, logos, brands, watermarks, UI, computers, code, faces, or people. This is decorative atmosphere, not a representation of the portfolio owner's actual home.

## Illustrations and fonts

Project illustrations are decorative HTML/CSS artwork. They are not screenshots, production interfaces, financial instruments or redeemable vouchers. No private product designs or customer information are published.

Fonts are Latin subsets of Google Fonts' Cormorant Garamond (normal and italic) and DM Sans, converted to WOFF2 for local delivery. Source families: <https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond> and <https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans>. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included in `public/fonts/`. Font loading requires no requests to Google.
